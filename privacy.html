Privacy Policy – Mochibe Mystery

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. About this service

Mochibe Mystery is a personal workflow used to create and prepare original German-language mystery and thriller videos for social media publication.

2. Information processed

The workflow may process story text, generated narration, subtitle files, video files, and publication metadata such as captions. These files are stored and processed on the operator's computer unless a connected service is used.

3. Connected services

If connected services such as OpenAI or TikTok are used, relevant text, media, account authorization data, or publication metadata may be transmitted to those services to perform the requested functions. Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Data sharing and retention

Data is not intentionally sold. Files may remain on the operator's computer until deleted. Retention and deletion practices of connected services are subject to their respective policies and settings.

5. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect account credentials, API keys, access tokens, and locally stored files. Secrets should never be published in this repository.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details provided in the app's official contact information.
